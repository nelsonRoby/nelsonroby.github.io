Cointrip

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective date: 21 October 2026

Overview

Cointrip (“we”, “the app”) is an offline-capable travel currency converter. Your privacy is designed in: the app does not require an account and does not collect personal or sensitive user data.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share personal information, identifiers, financial information, location, contacts, photos, or usage analytics. There are no third-party advertising SDKs and no tracking pixels in the app.

Network requests

The app makes requests to https://api.frankfurter.dev to download public exchange-rate reference data published by the European Central Bank. Requests contain only the currency codes and dates needed to fetch rates; no personal data is sent.

Data stored on your device

  • Cached exchange rates and rate history (stored locally so the app works offline).
  • Your preferences: favorite currency pairs, default currencies, decimal precision and theme.

All of this data remains on your device and is removed automatically when the app is uninstalled. We never upload it.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used solely to refresh exchange rates.
  • Boot completed / wake lock / foreground service — used by the optional background scheduler to keep cached rates current; no data leaves the device beyond the rate requests described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Financial note

Cointrip is an informational calculator only. It does not execute currency exchange, payments, transfers, or any financial transaction. Displayed rates are indicative reference rates and may differ from rates offered by banks, exchange kiosks or card issuers.
