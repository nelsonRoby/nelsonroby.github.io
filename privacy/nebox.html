NEBox

Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective date: October 28, 2026 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

01Summary概要

NEBox measures your network. You run the measurement; the app reports the result on screen. The maintainers of NEBox collect no personal data, operate no backend, and have no access to anything your device reports. Where a measurement unavoidably involves a third-party server — a speed-test node or a WHOIS registry, for example — that server necessarily sees your IP address and the query you asked for. Section 5 lists every server NEBox contacts and exactly what is sent.

NEBox 用来测量你的网络。测量由你发起，结果直接显示在屏幕上。NEBox 维护者不收集任何个人数据，不运营后端服务，也无法看到设备上的任何结果。少数功能不可避免地会访问第三方服务器（例如测速节点或 WHOIS 注册局），这类服务器必然会看到你的 IP 地址和你发起的查询。第 5 节逐项列出了会访问的所有服务器及发送内容。

02What we collect我们收集什么

Nothing. The NEBox maintainers do not collect, transmit, sell, share, rent, or monetise any personal data, device data, or usage data. There is no project-operated server that your measurements, history, or diagnostics are uploaded to. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no fingerprinting code.

什么都没有。NEBox 维护者不收集、不上传、不出售、不共享、不出租、不变现任何个人数据、设备数据或使用数据。本项目没有任何服务器会接收你的测量结果、历史记录或诊断信息。应用不包含广告 SDK、分析 SDK、崩溃上报 SDK 或任何指纹识别代码。

03Data processed on your device在设备本地处理的数据

The following is read from your device and shown to you. It is processed locally and is not uploaded by NEBox.

以下信息由应用从设备读取并展示给你，全部在本地处理，NEBox 不会将其上传到任何地方。

  • Wi-Fi scan results — SSID, BSSID, signal strength (dBm), link speed, band and channel.
  • Local network state — interface addresses, gateway and DNS server addresses, DHCP information.
  • Measurement results — ping, traceroute, MTR, speed test, DNS answers, WHOIS records, TCP port and TLS certificate check results.
  • Public IP address — fetched only when you open the “My IP” tool, and only from the service listed in section 5.
  • Run history — summaries of ping and speed test runs, stored on the device (section 6).
  • Wi-Fi 扫描结果 —— SSID、BSSID、信号强度（dBm）、链路速率、频段与信道。
  • 本地网络状态 —— 网卡地址、网关与 DNS 服务器地址、DHCP 信息。
  • 测量结果 —— ping、traceroute、MTR、测速、DNS 解析结果、WHOIS 注册信息、TCP 端口与 TLS 证书检测结果。
  • 公网 IP 地址 —— 仅在你打开「My IP」工具时获取，且只来自第 5 节列出的服务。
  • 运行历史 —— ping 与测速记录的摘要，保存在设备本地（见第 6 节）。

05Third-party servers第三方服务器

Some tools have to reach a server to answer your question. NEBox adds no identifiers, cookies, or tracking parameters to those requests. The server you contact will see your IP address and the query you typed — this is inherent to how the Internet works, and it applies to any of these tools.

部分工具必须访问服务器才能给出结果。NEBox 不会在这些请求中添加任何标识符、Cookie 或跟踪参数。你访问的服务器会看到你的 IP 地址和你输入的查询内容 —— 这是网络本身的机制，与具体工具无关。

06Local storage, retention and deletion本地存储、保留与删除

Ping history and speed test history are saved in the app's private DataStore inside NEBox's own sandbox. Each entry holds the target host, the measurement summary and a timestamp. This data never leaves your device through NEBox. You can erase it at any time with the Clear control in the Ping and Speed Test history lists, or by uninstalling the app.

Ping 与测速历史保存在 NEBox 私有沙箱内的 DataStore 中。每条记录包含目标主机、测量摘要与时间戳。这些数据不会经由 NEBox 离开你的设备。你可以随时在 Ping 与测速的历史列表中点击 Clear 清除，或卸载应用来删除。

If Android's system backup is enabled for your device, Android may include NEBox's local data in your device backup, which is stored by your device manufacturer or Google according to your device backup settings. That backup is outside NEBox's control — you can review or disable it in your device's system backup settings.

如果你的设备开启了 Android 系统备份，NEBox 的本地数据可能被包含进设备备份中，由设备厂商或 Google 按你的备份设置保存。这部分不受 NEBox 控制，你可以在设备的系统备份设置中查看或关闭。

07Sharing, sale and your rights共享、出售与你的权利

NEBox does not share, sell, rent, trade or otherwise disclose data with third parties, advertisers, data brokers or analytics providers. There is no data to disclose, because none is collected.

NEBox 不会与第三方、广告主、数据经纪商或分析服务商共享、出售、出租、交换或以其他方式披露任何数据。由于根本不收集数据，也就没有可披露的数据。

Because NEBox holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export, restrict or erase on our side. Under the GDPR, the CCPA and comparable laws, no data subject request can produce a response, because no such data exists. You can of course erase the local data described in section 6 at any time from the app itself.

由于 NEBox 不持有任何关于你的个人数据，因此不存在可查询、更正、导出、限制或删除的数据。在 GDPR、CCPA 及同类法律下，相关主体请求不会得到实质回应，因为这类数据并不存在。第 6 节中的本地数据你可以随时在应用内自行清除。

08Children儿童

NEBox is a utility for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). Because NEBox collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

NEBox 是面向成年人的实用工具，不以 13 周岁以下（或你所在司法辖区对应年龄）儿童为目标人群。由于 NEBox 不收集任何个人数据，自然也不会从儿童处收集数据。

09Security安全

NEBox stores nothing outside your device's private app sandbox, and requests to third-party services use HTTPS wherever the service supports it. The WHOIS tool is the exception: WHOIS runs over plain TCP port 43 because the protocol has no encrypted transport, and the WHOIS servers you query will log that you looked up a given domain.

NEBox 除设备私有应用沙箱外不存储任何数据；对第三方服务的请求在服务支持的情况下均使用 HTTPS。WHOIS 工具是例外：WHOIS 协议本身没有加密传输层，只能走明文 TCP 43 端口，你查询的 WHOIS 服务器会记录你查询过某个域名。
